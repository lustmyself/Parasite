======================================

ParasiteOffline.exe is a modified version of Deceive
(https://github.com/molenzwiebel/Deceive), Copyright (C) molenzwiebel and
Deceive's contributors. Parasite renamed what users see, removed its
first-launch messages and tray icon, and lets Parasite read and set the status.
